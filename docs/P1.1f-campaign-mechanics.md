# P1.1f — Campaign Frame & Supplemental Campaign Mechanics

Source: **Daggerheart SRD 2.0 (2026-08-25)**.

This pass covers the SRD material after adversaries/environments and before the Domain Card appendix:

- **The Witherwild** campaign frame, printed pp. 184–189.
- **Supplemental Campaign Mechanics**, printed pp. 190–205.

## Canonical targets

The Witherwild is stored as a single `campaign_frame` entity with stable sub-section IDs.

Supplemental mechanics are stored as 11 stable top-level entities:

1. Faction Tracking
2. Everyday Hero Starting Equipment
3. Feasts
4. Grimdark Campaigns
5. Tech-Based Campaigns
6. Western Campaigns
7. Colossal Adversaries
8. Floating Magic School Campaigns
9. Fairy Tale Campaigns
10. Monster Hunting Campaigns
11. Hex Crawl Campaigns

This first pass locks identity and source text. Mechanics can then be normalized per subsystem without changing canonical IDs.
