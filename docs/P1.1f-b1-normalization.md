# P1.1f-b1 — Supplemental mechanics normalization

This pass normalizes the five system-heavy SRD 2.0 supplemental mechanics while preserving the raw `rules_text`.

Targets:

- Faction Tracking
- Feasts
- Colossal Adversaries
- Monster Hunting Campaigns
- Hex Crawl Campaigns

Design rule: **do not duplicate entities already extracted elsewhere**. In particular, Monster Hunting weapons remain in the equipment collection and are referenced from the normalized campaign mechanic.

`Faction Tracking` is structural-only in b1. We deliberately avoid inferring track semantics from incidental numbers until its exact SRD structure is validated independently.
