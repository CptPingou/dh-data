# P1.1f-b2 — Remaining Supplemental normalization

Targets:

- Everyday Hero Starting Equipment
- Grimdark Campaigns
- Tech-Based Campaigns
- Western Campaigns
- Floating Magic School Campaigns
- Fairy Tale Campaigns

This pass preserves `rules_text` and adds a stable `mechanics` layer.

Design constraints:

- Western weapons are linked to the already extracted supplemental weapon collection instead of duplicated.
- Everyday Hero is normalized conservatively as a starting-equipment variant.
- Fairy Tale is normalized structurally first; no unsupported semantics are inferred.
- Grimdark, Tech-Based and Floating Magic School receive deterministic mechanical fields where the SRD states them explicitly.
