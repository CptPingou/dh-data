# P1.0 — SRD 2.0 identity index

This patch is meant to be MERGED into the existing `daggerheart-data` directory on the NAS.

## What is canonical in this pass

The official SRD 2.0 is now the public baseline.

Confirmed from the official SRD:
- 10 domains;
- 13 classes;
- 24 ancestries;
- 15 communities;
- 6 Hope & Fear transformations;
- 18 Core subclass identities;
- Assassin and Brawler subclass identities;
- the supplemental campaign-mechanics sections, including Monster Hunting and Feasts.

## Deliberate stop

This is an identity-index pass, not yet the full mechanical extraction.

Large collections are staged for P1.1/P1.2:
- domain cards;
- weapons;
- armor;
- loot;
- consumables;
- beastforms;
- adversaries;
- environments.

`registry/unresolved.json` records anything that has not been established safely enough to canonize.

The Warlock/Witch subclass mapping is deliberately left unresolved rather than guessed from an ambiguous extraction chunk.
