# P1.2 — Global SRD 2.0 completeness audit

This pass is the integration gate between extraction/normalization and adapters.

It validates the canonical SRD 2.0 layer as a whole:

- 13 classes
- 26 unique subclasses
- 24 ancestries
- 15 communities
- 10 domains
- 6 transformations
- 210 domain cards, 21 per domain
- 69 armor entries
- 120 items
- 120 consumables
- 24 beastforms
- 358 fixed weapon variants:
  - 302 standard
  - 12 combat wheelchairs
  - 44 supplemental
- 264 adversaries
- 47 environments
- 1 campaign frame / 14 Witherwild sections
- 11 supplemental campaign mechanics

## Integrity gates

P1.2 also checks:

- required canonical files exist;
- stable IDs are unique inside and across canonical collections;
- known tier distributions match the SRD-derived targets;
- class → domain references resolve when those references are present in the index data;
- all 11 supplemental mechanics have received their P1.1f normalization layer.

Historical/index helper files may overlap semantically. In particular, subclass coverage is audited as the semantic union of the original index and the Hope & Fear index rather than by summing both files.

A **GREEN** result means the neutral SRD layer is structurally ready for adapter work.
